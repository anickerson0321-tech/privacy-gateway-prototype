Help test an early privacy-tool concept

This is a short, self-guided product concept test. It is not a real security product yet, and it does not upload, scan, or process any of your files.

What is the idea?

Privacy Gateway is a concept for a local-first tool that would help people remove or anonymize sensitive information from work documents before using the remaining content with an approved AI tool or sharing it externally.

Important: Please do not use a real confidential document. The prototype uses a made-up sample document only. We are testing whether the idea and workflow make sense—not whether the technology works yet.

How to test it

1
